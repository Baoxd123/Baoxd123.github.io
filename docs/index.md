## **Biography**

Hello! My name is Xingda, or you can call me Eric.

Currently, I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Computing at the University of Georgia. I'm a member of the Network Intelligence and Security (NIS) Lab, where I'm fortunately working with Professor Roberto Perdisci.

Before joining UGA, I was a Software Engineer at Stellar Services, a company located in New York.
